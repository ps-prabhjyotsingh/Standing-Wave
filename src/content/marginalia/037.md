Habits collect at the ends of things. A sentence in the middle has to answer to the one
before it and the one after; a last sentence only has to sound like a last sentence, and
the hand reaches for one that has sounded right before. So a writer's tics are easiest to
find by reading only the endings, several pieces in a row, where they sit in a stack.
