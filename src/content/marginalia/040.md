C05's placard pictures the pure-chance counter passing a hundred million while its best
guess still has four letters right. The arithmetic is kinder to chance than that. Four
matching letters turn up in about fifty tries, inside the first frame. By a hundred million
the record usually stands at ten, more than a third of the phrase, and the readout looks
like progress. That is the better picture. Each further letter costs at least ten times the
last, so the record creeps up by orders of magnitude while the left column finishes in
about five seconds. Both columns keep their best. Only one is descended from it.
