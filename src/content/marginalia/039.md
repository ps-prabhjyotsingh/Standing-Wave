The shape C03 opens with is, by its own formula, a centre and four circles: two for the
ellipse, two for the four-beat wave. Five terms would trace it exactly. The piece cannot
find them. Before the transform it respaces the points evenly by distance along the curve,
and the formula's pen never moved at an even speed, so the same outline arrives as more than
a hundred circles and is still a couple of pixels off at forty-one. The circles were never
in the shape. They are in how the pen walks it, and the same line walked differently is a
different sum.
