C03 says every shape you can draw is a sum of circles turning at steady speeds: not
approximately, exactly, in the limit. That is true, and the word holding it up is *draw*.
In 1873 Paul du Bois-Reymond built a continuous curve whose Fourier series fails to
converge at one point; add as many circles as you like and the pen never settles there.
That curve has infinite length, so no hand will ever trace it. A pointer leaves a path of
finite length, and for those the circles converge everywhere along it. The claim is exact
because a hand can only make the tame cases.
