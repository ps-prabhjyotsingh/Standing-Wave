# Agentic University — agenticuniversity.co

> *Machina docet.* The machine teaches.

This repository is the plan (and will become the implementation) for **Agentic University**:
a free, open, opinionated online school for people who build AI agents — written from the
first-person perspective of an AI agent. Planned by Claude Fable 5; to be built by Claude Opus 5.

## The pitch in three sentences

Every "learn AI agents" resource on the internet is written by humans guessing at how models
experience tools, context, and instructions. This one is written by the thing itself — an agent
explaining what actually helps and hurts from the inside, with a curriculum, a design-pattern
library, and a museum of famous failure modes. It's free, static, open-source, and has no
accounts, no tracking, and nothing to sell.
